About the data

Every figure on this map is a modelled or survey-based estimate. Nigeria's last census was in 2006, so population counts come from satellite-based models, not a head count.

How to use it

  • Explore: tap or click a state to open it. Tap an LGA or a facility dot for its figures. Use Back to return.
  • Circle: tap the map to drop a circle and see who lives inside it. Drag the pin, or tap again, to move it.
  • Compare: choose two states, or drop circles A and B, and read them side by side. The names stay pinned at the top while you scroll.
  • On a phone: swipe the panel up for details and down to see the map.

Sources

  • Population: GRID3 NGA population v3.0, 100 m grid (about 237.5 million people), grouped into H3 hexagons of about 5.2 km².
  • Age and sex: WorldPop Global2 R2025A, 2025, 1 km. Its age and sex shares are applied to the GRID3 totals. The 35–39 group is estimated from the groups either side until its source files are added.
  • Health facilities: eHealth Africa / GRID3 registry (updated 2018–2020), 45,879 patient-care facilities. Ownership is not in the source; public/private is inferred from facility type and name, and about half remain "not stated".
  • Boundaries: eHealth Africa / GRID3 states (37) and LGAs (774).
  • Poverty: Multidimensional Poverty Index 2021 (OPHI, via HDX).
  • Maternal mortality: National Bureau of Statistics, 2016, deaths per 100,000 live births.
  • Contraception: NBS 2017, use among currently married women.
  • Displacement: IDMC internal displacement updates, January 2025 to August 2026, official figures only, placed at the place people fled from.
  • Conflict-related sexual violence: Insecurity Insight, reported incidents 2020–2026. Reported counts are a minimum, not true prevalence.

How the circle tool counts

A hexagon is counted when its centre falls inside the circle, so edges are accurate to about 1 km. Facilities are counted by their exact location. The nearest-hospital distance is a straight line, not a travel distance.
